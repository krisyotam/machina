# ~/edu

Coursework. Lectures. Assigned reading. The trail of a class, not
a personal investigation.

Shared primitives (hosts/SSH, subagent CLIs, models.env) live in
`~/.config/model/`. Read `~/.config/model/README.md` on launch and
append a row to `~/.local/state/model/log.csv` when the session ends.

## Put here

One directory per course or curriculum:
`~/edu/<course>/`. Keep the professor's materials distinct from
your notes if both exist.

## Put somewhere else

- A personal research write-up → `~/research`
- A program you are writing for yourself → `~/src`
- A book that is not assigned → `~/lit`

## Rules

- Never delete `~/edu`.
- Do not mix this with `~/research`. Coursework has a syllabus;
  research does not.
- Do not create `bin/` or `dev/` in here.
